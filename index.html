Personal Assistant

A private, single-user productivity assistant.

Personal Assistant is a self-hosted application built and used by one person. It reads its owner's Google Calendar and Google Tasks to answer questions about their schedule and to produce a single daily summary — deliberately one summary a day rather than a stream of notifications.

It is reached from an Apple Watch, which shows the daily summary and a few quick read-only views, and from a phone or browser for longer conversations.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads calendar events to answer questions like "what's on tomorrow?" and to build the daily summary.
  • Reads tasks to show what is due.
  • Creates and completes tasks when the owner explicitly asks it to.

It does not read email, contacts, files, or photos, and does not request access to them.

Who it is for

Its owner, and nobody else. It is not a product, has no other users, is not offered as a service, and is not accepting sign-ups. It runs entirely in its owner's own private Amazon Web Services account.

How data is handled

Calendar and task data stays in the owner's own AWS account, encrypted at rest. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, and the application contains no analytics or tracking of any kind. The privacy policy describes this in full, including which third-party services process data and how long it is kept.
